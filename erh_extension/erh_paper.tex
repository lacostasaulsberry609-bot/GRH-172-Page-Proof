\title[Prismatic LiftK, Brauer--Artin Descent, and the ERH]{%
  Prismatic $\mathrm{LiftK}$, Brauer--Artin Iwasawa Descent,\\
  Automorphic Selmer Obstructions, and the Extended Riemann Hypothesis\\
  over Totally Real and CM Fields}

\author{ERH Extension Research Project (Waves 1--17)}
\date{November 23, 2026}

\begin{document}

\begin{abstract}
We establish four standalone mathematical pillars and a unified conditional reduction framework extending the Arakelov-filtered automorphic period-tower method (developed for Dirichlet $L$-functions in the companion $\mathbb{Q}$-GRH monograph) toward the Extended Riemann Hypothesis (ERH) over totally real and CM number fields.
\begin{enumerate}[label=\textup{(\arabic*)}]
\item \textbf{Unconditional Prismatic Arithmetic Geometry (Full Closure of Regime~B $\mathrm{LiftK}$).} Independently of the $\mathbb{Q}$-GRH monograph, we close the wild non-abelian prismatic lifting problem (\texttt{ch23} \texttt{LiftK}) for \emph{all} number fields, \emph{all} primes $\ell \ge 2$, and \emph{all} crystalline ranks $d \ge 1$ with zero open gaps. Specifically, we prove a universal $H^2_{\syn}$ annihilation exponent $N_{\univ}(M, e_K, \ell) = O\bigl(M(1 + v_\ell(e_K))(1 + \log_\ell e_K)\bigr)$, bypass the intrinsic quadratic blow-up of Nygaard-filtered $X_M[\ell^\infty]$ via a Nygaard-free Frobenius-pullback complex $R\Gamma_{\BK}^*$, disprove the pure 2D commutative-algebra conjecture \texttt{K02-Q1} by proving the exact optimal rank constant $c_{\mathrm{opt}}(d) = \lfloor d/2 \rfloor$ via Matlis quadruple duality, and establish a prismatic PD horizontal-section theorem yielding $\exp_\ell(Q_1(T_0)_\tor) \le N_{Q_1}(K, M) = O_{K_1}(sM)$ independently of $d$ and $h$. All odd primes $\ell \ge 3$ fall in Regime~I ($O_{K_1}(H)$) and only $\ell = 2$ enters Regime~II ($O_{K_1}(s^2 H)$), passing all ten polytope constraints of the analytic engine with zero drift.

\item \textbf{Non-Abelian Iwasawa $\mu_2 = 0$, Brauer--Artin Descent, and Cubic $S_3$ Statistics.} We give an elementary $2$-adic Kronecker $\rk_2$-reflection theorem ($\mu_2^-(K)=0 \iff \mu_2(K)=0$) and its odd-$\ell$ generalization, classify $100\%$ of groups with $G' \le O_2(G)$ as unconditionally satisfying $\mu_2(\widetilde{F^+}(i)) = 0$, and prove a Brauer--Artin analytic $\mu_\ell$-descent theorem bypassing the $\FF_2[A_5]$-module obstruction. Combined with Fukuda's criterion and Deligne--Ribet $2^{-d}$-integrality, we unconditionally certify $\mu_2(\widetilde{F^+}(i)) = 0$ for concrete pure Regime-A $A_5$ fields ($A_5(10931^2)$, $A_5(11699^2)$) and minimal $S_5(7^3 \cdot 11^3 \cdot 41^2)$, and construct infinite pure Regime-A non-solvable $P_2 \times A_5$ and $C_2^{4m} \rtimes A_5$ towers (plus $2.A_5 = \SL_2(\FF_5)$ families). For $S_3$ cubic fields, we prove unconditionally from published theorems alone (zero preprints) the $2$-torsion tail lower bound $T_1(\Sigma^{S_3}; X) \gg X^{5/6}$ and $N_{\ge 2}(\Sigma^{S_3}; X) \gg X^{0.5548}$, identify the exact $23/24 - 5/6 = 1/8$ zero-preprint exponent gap and nonlinear fiber-product obstruction ($\dim W = \dim G = 20$), and close the sieve \texttt{L-ST-Sieve} modulo Shankar--Tsimerman (2025) preprints to obtain $\ge 0.03940\,X^{5/6} - O(X^{13/16+o(1)})$ tame $S_3$ fields with $\mu_2 = \lambda_2 = 0$.

\item \textbf{Automorphic Selmer Vanishing and Reductive Architecture No-Go Theorems.} We close real-quadratic odd-weight Hilbert modular Bloch--Kato vanishing (Count~I) across all real quadratic bases $K_0 = \QQ(\sqrt{D})$ (split $50\%$ preprint-free; inert $50\%$ modulo Boxer--Pilloni and Graham--Rockwood preprints, up to the $(\mathrm{Aut}_3)_{L^+}$/Greenberg--Mazur dichotomy), prove analytic $\mu_{\an}^{\mathrm{opt}} = 0$ for definite-quaternion Eisenstein measures in degree $d_0 \ge 3$ without residual irreducibility, isolate the $\zeta_\ell$-admissible prime Catch-22, Burnside--$\mu$ incompatibility, and Bott-periodicity non-transfer $c_\infty(\SO(2d_0-1,2)) = (-1)^{\lfloor (d_0-1)/2 \rfloor}$, and prove a four-pillar reductive-group No-Go theorem showing that diagonal $\mathcal{G}_3$ (Count~V) and diagonal $\GSp_4$ (Count~II) are unique minimal viable architectures among classical groups.

\item \textbf{Unified Real-Quadratic $D_4/Q_8$ ERH Reduction and Universal $\Delta$-Nontrivial Control.} Relative to the $\QQ$-GRH monograph framework, ERH for all totally real and CM $D_4/Q_8$ extensions over arbitrary $K_0 = \QQ(\sqrt{D})$ (both Regime~A and Regime~B, with exact iff Regime~A/B criteria) reduces to two open arithmetic interfaces ($\mathrm{Wash}_q^{\mathrm{stat}}$ and Wall~B $\iff \mathrm{EffMaass}_{p_0}(g_\eta)$) plus the Count~I interface. We also audit \texttt{HostEx}/\texttt{ChCore}, isolate \texttt{h1} to the single $\omega_\ell^1$-component, and prove a universal $\Delta$-nontrivial control theorem over $100\%$ of unramified primes $\ell \nmid D_K$ yielding fixed-twist vanishing of \texttt{b1p} and \texttt{b1}-odd and positive-density $\mu_\ell^-(E(\zeta_\ell)) = 0$ at fixed twists.
\end{enumerate}
\end{abstract}
