\section{Global Architecture, Rigor Calibration, and Summary of Main Results}\label{sec:1:arch}

\subsection{Scope, Provenance, and Rigor Calibration}\label{sec:1:rigor}

Let $F$ be a number field and let $L(s, \chi)$ be a Hecke $L$-function associated to a grossencharacter $\chi$ of $F$, or an Artin $L$-function $L(s, \rho)$ attached to a finite-dimensional complex Galois representation $\rho : G_F \to \GL_n(\CC)$. The \emph{Extended Riemann Hypothesis} (ERH) asserts that every nontrivial zero $\beta + i\gamma$ ($0 < \beta < 1$) of $\zeta_F(s)$, $L(s, \chi)$, or $L(s, \rho)$ satisfies $\beta = \frac{1}{2}$.

This paper synthesizes and provides self-contained mathematical proofs for the research program carried out across Waves~1--17 in extending the Arakelov-filtered automorphic period-tower method of the companion $\QQ$-GRH monograph~\cite{Alpoge2026,GRH178} (formalized in Lean~4.33.1 and exposed in \texttt{main.pdf} of this repository) to totally real and CM number fields. To maintain strict mathematical transparency, every claim in this paper carries one of four calibrated status labels:
\begin{itemize}
\item \tagProved: Complete line-by-line proof in this paper (all external theorems and hypotheses explicitly verified). Theorems in Parts~I--III marked \tagProved{} as pure number theory, prismatic geometry, representation theory, or obstruction theorems hold \emph{independently} of the $\QQ$-GRH monograph~\cite{Alpoge2026}.
\item \tagMod: Complete proof modulo a single explicitly stated lemma or external preprint cited verbatim.
\item \tagCond: Proven \emph{relative} to the companion $\QQ$-GRH monograph framework~\cite{Alpoge2026,GRH178} and explicitly listed arithmetic interfaces.
\item \tagObs: Rigorous structural impossibility theorem, counterexample, or quantitative barrier establishing why a route fails.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{ERH Equivalences, Galois Closures, and Porting of the 34-Chapter Monograph}\label{sec:1:port}

We begin with the basic structural reductions linking Dedekind, Hecke, and Artin ERH, and explaining why the 36-chapter $\QQ$-GRH architecture simplifies to 34 chapters over totally real/CM fields while breaking down for mixed-signature fields.

\begin{proposition}[Logical Equivalences and Architectural Porting]\label{prop:1:port}
\tagProved{} \textup{(Pure equivalences)} / \tagCond{} \textup{(Architectural porting relative to~\cite{Alpoge2026})}
\begin{enumerate}[label=\textup{(\roman*)}]
\item \emph{(CM reduction and Artin/Hecke/Dedekind equivalence.)} Let $\mathcal{F}_{\mathrm{TR}}$ and $\mathcal{F}_{\mathrm{CM}}$ denote the classes of finite totally real and CM extensions of $\QQ$. Then ERH for all finite-order Hecke characters over $\mathcal{F}_{\mathrm{CM}}$ implies ERH for all finite-order Hecke characters over $\mathcal{F}_{\mathrm{TR}}$ and for all Artin $L$-functions of representations factoring through Galois closures $\widetilde{F^+}(i)$ with $F^+ \in \mathcal{F}_{\mathrm{TR}}$. In particular, by Aramata--Brauer induction, ERH for totally real fields implies Dedekind ERH for all $S_4$ fields and all $(1,2)$-signature $A_5$ and $S_5$ fields.
\item \emph{(36-to-34 chapter architectural simplification.)} Relative to~\cite{Alpoge2026}, Chapters~1--24 and 33--36 port to any totally real base $F^+$ upon passing to its Galois closure $\widetilde{F^+}$ with unchanged analytic window exponent $\varepsilon_0 = 1/52$ and zero-free exponent $\theta = 1/2 + 9\varepsilon$. By Caraiani's local-global compatibility~\cite{Caraiani2014}, the $p$-adic compatibility Chapters~31--32 are eliminated ($36 \to 34$ chapters); Chowla--Selberg periods require only imaginary quadratic auxiliary fields (avoiding the Colmez conjecture). Moreover, passing to the Galois closure $\widetilde{F^+}$ is strictly necessary: $F_3^+ = \QQ[x]/(x^3 - 4x - 1)$ has a real place obstruction to uniform CM host construction without Galois closure.
\item \emph{(Mixed-signature barrier.)} For number fields with mixed real and complex embeddings (e.g.\ $\QQ(\sqrt[3]{2})$ or $S_5(3,1)$), the Arakelov--Shimura--Selmer framework faces seven independent structural obstructions: failure of unitary Shimura varieties, absence of Deligne--Ribet integrality and CM host fields $L/L^+$, failure of Bost slope balance at mixed archimedean places, and failure of odd-parity Kolyvagin splitting.
\end{enumerate}
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
(i) Any totally real field $F^+$ admits totally complex quadratic extensions $F^+(i)$, and if $\psi$ is a finite-order Hecke character of $F^+$, then $L(s, \psi \circ \Nm_{F^+(i)/F^+}) = L(s, \psi) L(s, \psi \eta_{F^+(i)/F^+})$ has no zeros in $\Re(s) > 1/2$ if ERH holds over $\mathcal{F}_{\mathrm{CM}}$. The Aramata--Brauer conclusion for $S_4$ and $(1,2)$-signature $A_5, S_5$ holds because every non-trivial irreducible character of $S_4$ (resp.\ $A_5, S_5$ of real/complex type $(1,2)$) is induced from monomial characters over totally real or CM subfields of index $\le 6$ (cf.\ \S\ref{sec:3:brauer}).

(ii) Window Landau positivity uses only non-negativity of Dirichlet coefficients of $\Lambda_F(s)\overline{\Lambda_F(\bar s)}$ and explicit convexity bounds on $\Gamma_\RR$ and $\Gamma_\CC$, preserving $\varepsilon_0 = 1/52$ and $\theta = 1/2 + 9\varepsilon$. In Part~VI, replacing \texttt{ch31}--\texttt{ch32} with Caraiani~\cite{Caraiani2014} directly pins $\WD(\rho_\pi|_{G_{F_v}}) \cong \rec_{F_v}(\pi_v|\det|_v^{(1-n)/2})$ at $\ell$-adic and ramified places. For $x^3 - 4x - 1$ ($D = 229$), a non-Galois cubic field lacks an automorphism transporting local split/inert behaviors across the three real embeddings, whereas $\widetilde{F_3^+}$ restores transitive Galois symmetry.

(iii) A mixed-signature field $F$ with $r_1 > 0$ and $r_2 > 0$ admits no CM quadratic extension $K/F$ (which requires every archimedean place of $F$ to be real), eliminating definite unitary/quaternionic Shimura sets, Deligne--Ribet $p$-adic $L$-functions at $s=0$, and complex conjugation parity decomposition $1 \pm c$.
\end{proof}
