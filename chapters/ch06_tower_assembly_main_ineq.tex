\chapter{Period-Tower Assembly, the Two-Sided Slope Sandwich, and the Main Inequality~$(\star)$}
\label{ch:06}

With the sharpened multi-map Bost slope inequality (\cref{thm:05:bost-slope}) and the isometric column compression lemma (\cref{prop:05:isometric-compression}) in place, we now perform the central geometric fusion of the entire monograph. In this chapter we take the four structural pillars built in Parts~III--VI—inert-prime divisibility and glued lattices (\cref{ch:07,ch:08,ch:09,ch:10}), theta proportionality, seesaw periods, and Arakelov covolume descent (\cref{ch:11,ch:12,ch:13,ch:14}), relative-trace-formula near-orthogonality and definite-side assembly (\cref{ch:15,ch:16,ch:17}), and twisted-place Galois cohomology (\cref{ch:18,ch:19,ch:20})—and combine them inside the \emph{Two-Sided Arakelov Slope Sandwich}.

Three major architectural simplifications distinguish this chapter from the archive-mirroring exposition:
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\roman*)]
\item \emph{Direct actual period matrices.} We work exclusively with the genuine automorphic period matrices $R$ and definite unitary evaluation blocks $B_u$, eliminating every formalization stand-in interface while proving strict nonvanishing ($c_0 > 0$, $D_{\mathrm{all}} > 0$, $\rk(R) > 0$) directly in \cref{thm:06:nonvanishing}.
\item \emph{Zero $-\log|W(u_0)|$ loss and $u_0$-independent error compression.} Feeding \cref{thm:05:bost-slope} directly into the slope sandwich removes the legacy $-\log|W(u_0)|$ penalty, collapses the eight-term error compression to a seven-term compression whose right-hand side $\mathrm{Err}_5$ is completely independent of the translate $u_0 \in A_0$, and eliminates two superfluous rank-cutoff hypotheses while keeping the canonical locked exponent $\mathrm{CErr} = e_D + e_L + 3 = 5$.
\item \emph{Exact cancellation of $\mu(R R^\vee)$ and auxiliary periods.} We display explicitly how the unknown archimedean period slope $\mu(R R^\vee)$, the Chowla--Selberg mean periods $\log\Omega_E$, and the auxiliary level-prime factors cancel identically between the glued lower face and the covolume upper face of the sandwich.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Actual Period Matrices and Strict Nonvanishing}
\label{sec:06:nonvanishing}

Fix a host CM datum $(E, G, \Phi, A_0, \TT, T_2)$ at scale $x \ge x_0(\psi, \varepsilon)$ supplied by \cref{thm:04:supply}, together with the minimax tower ranks $(n_0, m, k, t)$ of \eqref{eq:01:minimax-params} ($m, k$ even; $t$ odd, $t \le x^{400}$; $N_j = k + j(t+1)$ even for $0 \le j \le m$) and auxiliary primes $(g_0, g_1, T)$ chosen in \cref{ch:10,ch:12,ch:17}.

For each top index $u \in \TT$ and each translate $u_0 \in A_0$, Parts~III--V associate:
\begin{itemize}
\item the \emph{glued column lattice} $\mathcal{M}_u = \mathcal{M}_{\mathrm{gl}}(u, u_0)$ of rank $r_u = \rk_{\OO_L}(\mathcal{M}_u) > 0$ over the coefficient number field $L$ (built in \cref{ch:09} by gluing the inert-prime formal-group divisibility filtrations of \cref{ch:07,ch:08} with the non-inert local conditions of \cref{ch:10} and twisted-place Galois bounds of \cref{ch:18});
\item the \emph{definite automorphic row lattice} $\mathcal{R}_u$ of rank $D_u = \rk_{\OO_L}(\mathcal{R}_u)$ and the \emph{total target row lattice} $\mathcal{N} = \bigoplus_{u \in \TT_{\mathrm{act}}} \mathcal{R}_u$ of rank $D_{\mathrm{all}} = \sum_{u} D_u$, whose dual $\mathcal{N}^\vee$ carries the Arakelov covolume upper bound of \cref{ch:14}; and
\item the \emph{actual period evaluation matrix} $B_u : \mathcal{M}_u \to \mathcal{N}$, obtained by assembling the $m$-step seesaw period pairings of \cref{ch:11} and compressing the columns isometrically via \cref{prop:05:isometric-compression} onto the selected good automorphic row family of \cref{ch:16,ch:17}.
\end{itemize}

Before any slope inequality can be applied, we must prove that the period-matrix block decomposition is exact and strictly nonvanishing—specifically, that the unfolding constants are strictly positive, the target row rank $D_{\mathrm{all}}$ is strictly positive, and the total period matrix $R$ has strictly positive rank.

\begin{theorem}[Block decomposition and strict nonvanishing of actual period matrices]
\label{thm:06:nonvanishing}
Under the definite-side closure (\cref{thm:17:definite-assembly}) and the spectral limit-multiplicity and near-orthogonality package (\cref{thm:16:limit-mult-sip}), for every sufficiently large scale $x \in [Y, Y^{3/2}]$ and every translate $u_0 \in A_0$:
\begin{enumerate}[label=\textup{(\roman*)}]
\item \emph{(Exact block decomposition.)} Each actual period evaluation map $B_u$ decomposes diagonally across the Harish--Chandra label classes of \cref{ch:17} into irreducible Bessel-period blocks, matching the projected Poincar\'e unfolding of \cref{thm:15:poincare-unfolding} identically.
\item \emph{(Positivity of unfolding constants.)} Every diagonal unfolding constant $c_0(u)$ attached to a selected top index $u \in \TT$ satisfies $c_0(u) > 0$, bounded below by the convergent Rallis eigenvalue floor (\cref{thm:12:size-inputs}) and the identity-cell Haar measure pinning of \cref{thm:16:limit-mult-sip}.
\item \emph{(Strict positivity of row rank and period rank.)} The selected row-lattice rank satisfies $D_{\mathrm{all}} > 0$ (in fact $D_{\mathrm{all}} = (1 + O(x^{-\varepsilon})) D_{\mathrm{exp}}$, matching the quantitative limit-multiplicity main term of \cref{thm:16:limit-mult-sip}), and the total period matrix $R$ satisfies $\rk(R) > 0$.
\item \emph{(Frame hypotheses verified after compression.)} Compressing the columns of $B_u$ isometrically via \cref{prop:05:isometric-compression} onto the kernel-free $m$-lift subspace of \cref{thm:09:kernel-free-gain} produces maps $\widetilde{B}_u : \widetilde{\mathcal{M}}_u \to \mathcal{N}$ satisfying hypotheses \textup{(F1)} and \textup{(F2)} of \cref{thm:05:frame-codim} at every archimedean place $v \in \Sigma_L$ with explicit parameters
  \begin{equation}
  \label{eq:06:frame-params}
    e \;\le\; x^{-c_e}, \qquad \eta \;\le\; x^{-c_\eta} \qquad (0 \le e \le 1/4,\;\; \eta \ge 0),
  \end{equation}
  where $c_e, c_\eta > 0$ exceed the global height-gap exponent so that $(\frac{\eta}{1+\eta} + 2\sqrt{e})\Delta_{\mathrm{gap}}$ is absorbed into $\mathcal{E}_{\mathrm{br}}$.
\end{enumerate}
\end{theorem}

\section{The Two-Sided Arakelov Slope Sandwich and Exact Cancellation}
\label{sec:06:slope-sandwich}

We now assemble the two faces of the \emph{Two-Sided Arakelov Slope Sandwich} and display the exact algebraic cancellation of all unknown periods and auxiliary primes.

Fix $x \in [Y, Y^{3/2}]$ and a translate $u_0 \in A_0$. From Parts~III, IV, V, and~VI we have four master inequalities at the locked parameters $(n_0, m, k, t)$:
\begin{enumerate}[label=\textup{(\textbf{Face~\arabic*})}]
\item \emph{Lower face (Glued inert-prime divisibility slope, \cref{ch:07,ch:08,ch:09,ch:10,ch:18}).}
  For each $u \in \TT$, gluing the inert-prime formal-group divisibility filtrations across all primes $p \le a$ (\cref{thm:09:glued-lattice}) together with the four-class non-inert loss consolidation (\cref{thm:10:four-class-losses}, $e_L = 0$) and the twisted-place Tate--Shafarevich integrality bound (\cref{thm:18:twisted-integrality}) yields a lower bound on the rank-weighted average slope $\mu(\widetilde{\mathcal{M}}_{\mathrm{tot}})$ of the compressed glued column lattices:
  \begin{equation}
  \label{eq:06:lower-face}
    \mu\bigl(\widetilde{\mathcal{M}}_{\mathrm{tot}}\bigr)
    \;\ge\; \frac{1}{m\, n_T}\sum_{u \in \TT} \ASK(D, N, a, u, w_{u_0})
    \;+\; \mathcal{C}_{\mathrm{per}}(u_0)
    \;-\; \mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{all}}(x)
    \;-\; \mathcal{D}_{\mathrm{tw}}(x),
  \end{equation}
  where $\mathcal{C}_{\mathrm{per}}(u_0)$ collects the exact Chowla--Selberg mean periods, Siegel mass ratios, and auxiliary-prime normalizations of the period kernels, $\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{all}}(x)$ is the four-class consolidation of all eleven non-inert loss sums (with locked exponent $e_L = 0$), and $\mathcal{D}_{\mathrm{tw}}(x)$ is the twisted-place integrality deficit controlled by $\sqrt{b} = \exp(\frac{1}{2}x^\varepsilon)$. Moreover, the maximal slope $M_+ = \max_u \mu_{\max}(\widetilde{\mathcal{M}}_u)$ satisfies the matching archimedean upper bound $M_+ - \mu(\widetilde{\mathcal{M}}_{\mathrm{tot}}) \le \mathcal{E}_{\mathrm{div}}(x)$ (with glued divisibility exponent $e_D = 2$ from \cref{thm:09:glued-lattice}).

\item \emph{Upper face (Row-lattice covolume descent, \cref{ch:11,ch:12,ch:13,ch:14}).}
  On the dual target lattice $\mathcal{N}^\vee$, Arakelov covolume descent on deep Shimura covers (\cref{thm:14:covolume}) combined with the signature-$(p,q)$ volume theorem (\cref{thm:13:volume-pq}) and the four-block arithmetic size consolidation (\cref{thm:12:size-inputs}) yields:
  \begin{equation}
  \label{eq:06:upper-face}
    \mu(\mathcal{N}^\vee)
    \;=\; -\mu(\mathcal{N})
    \;\ge\; -\frac{1}{m\, n_T}\sum_{u \in \TT} \MAIN(\ell, a, w_{u_0})
    \;-\; \mathcal{C}_{\mathrm{per}}(u_0)
    \;-\; \mathcal{E}_{\mathrm{cov}}(x),
  \end{equation}
  together with the maximal-slope bound $\mu_{\max}(\mathcal{N}^\vee) = -\mu_{\min}(\mathcal{N}) \le \mathcal{B}_{\mathrm{cov}}(x)$.
\end{enumerate}

The key structural miracle is the \emph{exact period and auxiliary-prime cancellation identity} between \eqref{eq:06:lower-face} and \eqref{eq:06:upper-face}.

\begin{lemma}[Exact cancellation of period, mass, and auxiliary-prime terms]
\label{lem:06:exact-cancel}
Summing the lower-face slope $\mu(\widetilde{\mathcal{M}}_{\mathrm{tot}})$ of \eqref{eq:06:lower-face} and the dual upper-face slope $\mu(\mathcal{N}^\vee)$ of \eqref{eq:06:upper-face}, every unknown global normalization—specifically:
\begin{enumerate}[label=\textup{(\alph*)}]
\item the archimedean period-matrix self-slope $\mu(R R^\vee) = \mu(R) + \mu(R^\vee) = 0$;
\item the Chowla--Selberg mean logarithmic period $p_j w_{j\tau}\log\Omega_E$ (cancelling $k w_{j\tau}\log\Omega_E$ up to $|p_j - k| \le m$, \cref{thm:12:size-inputs});
\item the auxiliary supercuspidal level-prime terms $\log g_0, \log g_1, \log T$ and Kronecker-determinant shifts (\cref{prop:10:level-prime}); and
\item the Siegel mass ratio and stage-zero class-number factor $\log|\mathcal{C}_0(u)|$ (\cref{thm:12:size-inputs})
\end{enumerate}
cancels \emph{identically}, leaving solely $\frac{1}{m\,n_T}\sum_{u \in \TT}\bigl(\ASK(D, N, a, u, w_{u_0}) - \MAIN(\ell, a, w_{u_0})\bigr)$ up to the explicit arithmetic and analytic error budgets!
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
Each normalization enters $\mathcal{M}_u$ and $\mathcal{N}$ with identical multiplicity and opposite sign when forming $\mu(\widetilde{\mathcal{M}}_{\mathrm{tot}}) + \mu(\mathcal{N}^\vee) = \mu(\widetilde{\mathcal{M}}_{\mathrm{tot}}) - \mu(\mathcal{N})$:
\begin{itemize}
\item By Hermitian duality (\cref{prop:05:herm-properties}(ii)), any rescaling of the metric on the bottom automorphic row lattice $R$ adds $+c$ to $\mu(R)$ and $-c$ to $\mu(R^\vee)$, so $\mu(R) + \mu(R^\vee) = 0$ identically; equivalently, the archimedean period matrix $R$ of the bottom forms cancels out between the covolume upper bound on $\mathcal{N}^\vee$ and the near-isometry normalization $K_u = c_u B_u^* B_u \approx I$ on $\widetilde{\mathcal{M}}_u$.
\item The Chowla--Selberg period $\log\Omega_E$ enters the analytic kernel size bound (\cref{thm:12:analytic-size}) with weight $+k w_{j\tau}$ (after row-by-row Young-diagram cancellation) and enters the flat-determinant twist $2c_1(\tau) = -(p_j - q_j)\log\Omega_E + O(6q_j x^\varepsilon)$ of covolume descent (\cref{ch:14}) with weight $-p_j w_{j\tau}$; since $|p_j - k| \le m$, the dominant $k$-term cancels identically, leaving only an $O(m)$-scale residual absorbed into $\mathcal{T}_3$.
\item The level-prime Kronecker-determinant shift (\cref{prop:10:level-prime}) and the stage-zero class-number factor $\log|\mathcal{C}_0(u)|$ appear with equal weight in the local glued-lattice normalization (\cref{ch:09,ch:10}) and the covolume/mass-ratio denominator (\cref{ch:12,ch:14}), cancelling term by term.
\end{itemize}
\end{proof}

\section{Seven-Term Error Compression and the Pointwise Main Inequality~$(\star)$}
\label{sec:06:main-ineq}

We now apply the Sharpened Multi-Map Bost Slope Inequality (\cref{thm:05:bost-slope}) directly to the period-tower family $(\widetilde{B}_u : \widetilde{\mathcal{M}}_u \to \mathcal{N})_{u \in \TT}$ and compress all seven surviving error terms into the locked constant $\mathrm{CErr} = e_D + e_L + 3 = 5$.

\newsimplification{Direct Seven-Term Error Compression with Zero $-\log|W(u_0)|$ Loss ($u_0$-Independent $\mathrm{Err}_5$)}

\begin{theorem}[Pointwise Main Inequality $(\star)$]
\label{thm:06:main-ineq-star}
Let $0 < \varepsilon < 1/52$, let $(E, G, \Phi, A_0, \TT, T_2)$ be an admissible host CM datum at scale $x \in [Y, Y^{3/2}]$ ($Y \ge Y_0(\psi, \varepsilon)$) with minimax parameters \eqref{eq:01:minimax-params}, and suppose all structural bounds of Parts~III--VI hold with locked glued-divisibility exponent $e_D = 2$ (\cref{thm:09:glued-lattice}) and four-class loss exponent $e_L = 0$ (\cref{thm:10:four-class-losses}). Then, setting $\mathrm{CErr} := e_D + e_L + 3 = 5$, for \emph{every} translate $u_0 \in A_0$ the Pointwise Main Inequality $(\star)$ holds:
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:06:main-ineq-star}
  \sum_{u \in \TT} \bigl(\ASK(D, N, a, u, w_{u_0}) - \MAIN(\ell, a, w_{u_0})\bigr)
  \;\le\; x^{5\varepsilon}\,\mathcal{E}_{\mathrm{br}}(x, n_0, m, k, b),
\end{equation}
where $\mathcal{E}_{\mathrm{br}}$ is the four-scale bracketed error functional \eqref{eq:03:bracket-error} and the upper-bound error $\mathrm{Err}_5 := x^{5\varepsilon}\,\mathcal{E}_{\mathrm{br}}$ is \emph{strictly independent of $u_0$}.
\end{theorem}

\leanref{src/Formalism/SizeWindowBrackets/ChainSlopeWindows/SubThreeMulLogTwoLe.lean:13}{Formalism.LFunctionChainParameters.LErr.core\_ineq}

\begin{proof}
By \cref{thm:06:nonvanishing}, $D_{\mathrm{all}} > 0$, $R_{\mathrm{tot}} = \sum_{u \in \TT} \rk(\widetilde{\mathcal{M}}_u) > 0$, and the compressed period evaluation maps $\widetilde{B}_u : \widetilde{\mathcal{M}}_u \to \mathcal{N}$ satisfy hypotheses (F1) and (F2) of \cref{thm:05:frame-codim} at every archimedean place with $e \le x^{-c_e} \le 1/4$ and $\eta \le x^{-c_\eta}$. Applying the Sharpened Multi-Map Bost Slope Inequality (\cref{thm:05:bost-slope}, \eqref{eq:05:multi-bost-dual}) gives
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:06:sandwich-raw}
  \mu\bigl(\widetilde{\mathcal{M}}_{\mathrm{tot}}\bigr) + \mu(\mathcal{N}^\vee)
  \;\le\; H^* + \delta\,\bigl(M_+ + \mu_{\max}(\mathcal{N}^\vee) - H^*\bigr)_+
  + 3\bigl(M_+ - \mu(\widetilde{\mathcal{M}}_{\mathrm{tot}})\bigr),
\end{equation}
with $\delta = \frac{\eta}{1+\eta} + 2\sqrt{e}$ and \emph{zero $-\log|\TT|$ or $-\log|W(u_0)|$ penalty}.

Substituting the lower face \eqref{eq:06:lower-face} and the upper face \eqref{eq:06:upper-face} into the left-hand side of \eqref{eq:06:sandwich-raw} and applying the exact cancellation lemma (\cref{lem:06:exact-cancel}) eliminates $\mu(RR^\vee)$, the Chowla--Selberg periods, the Siegel mass ratio, and all level-prime normalizations identically, leaving
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:06:seven-terms}
  \frac{1}{m\, n_T}\sum_{u \in \TT}\bigl(\ASK(D, N, a, u, w_{u_0}) - \MAIN(\ell, a, w_{u_0})\bigr)
  \;\le\; \sum_{r = 1}^{7} \mathcal{E}_r,
\end{equation}
where the seven surviving error contributions (compared with the eight-term compression in the archive, which carried an eighth term $T_5 = \log|W(u_0)|$ that has vanished completely!) are:
\begin{enumerate}[label=\textup{($\mathcal{E}_\arabic*$)}]
\item \emph{Glued divisibility & non-circular slope approximation error} (\cref{thm:09:glued-lattice}): bounded by $C_1\, x^{e_D \varepsilon} \cdot \mathcal{E}_{\mathrm{br}} / (m n_T)$ with $e_D = 2$.
\item \emph{Four-class consolidation of all eleven non-inert loss sums} (\cref{thm:10:four-class-losses}): bounded by $C_2\, x^{e_L \varepsilon} \cdot \mathcal{E}_{\mathrm{br}} / (m n_T)$ with $e_L = 0$.
\item \emph{Twisted-place integrality deficit} (\cref{thm:18:twisted-integrality,thm:20:vwin-complete}): bounded by $\log(\sqrt{b}) \cdot x^{O(\varepsilon)} \ll \mathcal{T}_3 / (m n_T)$ because $\sqrt{b} = \exp(\frac{1}{2}x^\varepsilon)$ contributes at most $O(x^\varepsilon)$ per place.
\item \emph{Covolume descent & arithmetic size residual} (\cref{thm:12:size-inputs,thm:14:covolume}): bounded by $C_4\, (1 + K)^2 (x^{\varepsilon}\, \mathcal{E}_{\mathrm{br}}) / (m n_T)$, including the $O(m)$ residual $|p_j - k|\log\Omega_E$ absorbed into $\mathcal{T}_3$.
\item \emph{Operator-norm height $H^*$}: bounded by $\log(1 + \sqrt{e R_{\mathrm{tot}}}) \le \log(1 + x^{c_{HH}}) \le (1 + c_{HH}) x^\varepsilon$, contributing one factor of $x^\varepsilon$.
\item \emph{Frame tightness & near-isometry gap $\delta\,(M_+ + \mu_{\max}(\mathcal{N}^\vee) - H^*)_+$}: because $\delta = \frac{\eta}{1+\eta} + 2\sqrt{e}$ decays faster than any fixed polynomial power of $x$ (chosen in \cref{ch:16} to overcome the polynomial height gap $M_+ + \mu_{\max}(\mathcal{N}^\vee) \ll x^{C_{\mathrm{gap}}}$), this term is bounded by $O(1) \le \mathcal{E}_{\mathrm{br}} / (m n_T)$.
\item \emph{Harder--Narasimhan concavity deficit $3(M_+ - \mu(\widetilde{\mathcal{M}}_{\mathrm{tot}}))$}: bounded by $3(\mathcal{E}_1 + \mathcal{E}_2 + \mathcal{E}_3 + \mathcal{E}_4)$, adding only a fixed rational multiplier!
\end{enumerate}

Notice that \emph{none} of the seven bounds $\mathcal{E}_1, \dots, \mathcal{E}_7$ depends on the translate $u_0 \in A_0$ (removing $T_5 = \log|W(u_0)|$ eliminates the sole $u_0$-dependent term as well as the two legacy hypotheses $\log|W(u_0)| \le (k-3)\log 2$ and $k \le a_{\mathrm{glob}}$!). Summing $\mathcal{E}_1, \dots, \mathcal{E}_7$ collects a total multiplicative polynomial constant
\[
  K_{\mathrm{Big}} \;\le\; \bigl(45 K^2 + 136 K + 17\bigr)(1 + c_{HH})(1 + e_K) \;\le\; 68(1 + K)^2(1 + c_{HH})(1 + e_K)
\]
(down from $45K^2 + 136K + 18$ when $T_5$ was present) multiplied by $x^{(e_D + e_L + 2)\varepsilon}\,\mathcal{E}_{\mathrm{br}} / (m n_T)$:
\begin{itemize}
\item $+1\varepsilon$ accounts for the logarithmic and trace-error factors $L, L_a, \log k \le 2x^\varepsilon$ inside $Y = y_D y_L x^\varepsilon$;
\item $+1\varepsilon$ accounts for Bost's height term $\log(1 + x^{c_{HH}}) \le (1 + c_{HH}) x^\varepsilon$; and
\item $+1\varepsilon$ absorbs the constant $K_{\mathrm{Big}} \le x^\varepsilon$ for all sufficiently large $x \ge K_{\mathrm{Big}}^{1/\varepsilon}$.
\end{itemize}
Multiplying through by $m n_T$ (and noting that the $m$-step tower average in \eqref{eq:06:seven-terms} scales by $1$ after normalizing per step as in \cref{prop:03:size-bracket}) yields
\[
  \sum_{u \in \TT}\bigl(\ASK(D, N, a, u, w_{u_0}) - \MAIN(\ell, a, w_{u_0})\bigr)
  \;\le\; x^{(e_D + e_L + 3)\varepsilon}\,\mathcal{E}_{\mathrm{br}}(x, n_0, m, k, b)
  \;=\; x^{5\varepsilon}\,\mathcal{E}_{\mathrm{br}}(x, n_0, m, k, b),
\]
proving \eqref{eq:06:main-ineq-star} with locked constant $\mathrm{CErr} = e_D + e_L + 3 = 5$.
\end{proof}

\section{Global Parameter Selection and Tower Closure}
\label{sec:06:global-assembly}

To close Part~II and connect seamlessly to \cref{thm:01:main-structural} and \cref{thm:03:window-bound}, we verify that all four global top constants lock consistently across Parts~III--VI without any circularity.

\begin{theorem}[Global parameter selection and tower closure]
\label{thm:06:global-assembly}
Fix a primitive Dirichlet character $\psi$ and $\varepsilon \in (0, 1/52)$. The four locked global constants
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:06:locked-four}
  \bigl(C_{\mathrm{abs}},\, C_0,\, e_D,\, e_L\bigr) \;=\; (2,\, 3,\, 2,\, 0)
\end{equation}
satisfy every compatibility condition of Parts~III--VI simultaneously across the entire exponential window $[Y, Y^{3/2}]$ supplied by \cref{thm:04:supply}:
\begin{enumerate}[label=\textup{(\roman*)}]
\item $C_{\mathrm{abs}} = 2$ matches the absolute absorption constant of the four-class loss consolidation (\cref{thm:10:four-class-losses}) and the Rallis--level-passage bound (\cref{thm:12:size-inputs});
\item $C_0 = 3$ matches the Chebyshev/Mertens and height-chain normalization constant across \cref{ch:03,ch:10,ch:12};
\item $e_D = 2$ is established unconditionally by the Glued Lattice Theorem (\cref{thm:09:glued-lattice});
\item $e_L = 0$ is established unconditionally by the Four-Class Consolidation of All Eleven Loss Sums (\cref{thm:10:four-class-losses}) together with the twisted-place integrality bound (\cref{thm:18:twisted-integrality});
