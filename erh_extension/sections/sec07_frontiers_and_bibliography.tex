\section{Dead-End Registry, Companion Artifacts, and Bibliography}\label{sec:7:end}

\subsection{Consolidated Registry of Ruled-Out and Refuted Routes}\label{sec:7:dead}

Across 11 rounds of independent adversarial auditing (Waves~8--17, uncovering and resolving $83$ technical defects across Waves~8--15 and Wave~17), the following routes were rigorously refuted or structurally blocked (\tagObs{}):
\begin{enumerate}[label=\textup{(\arabic*)}]
\item \emph{Mixed-signature number fields} (Prop.~\ref{prop:1:port}(iii)): seven foundational obstructions preclude porting the CM/unitary framework.
\item \emph{Regime~B classical tools} (\texttt{H02-1}): tame $C_{e_1}$ descent, fractional cyclotomic Wach rings (fail mod $\ell^2$), logarithmic crystalline pushforward, and log-\v{C}ech--Alexander fail in wild ramification.
\item \emph{Pure 2D lattice algebra \texttt{K02-Q1} for growing ranks} (Thm.~\ref{thm:2:matlis}): refuted for all absolute constants $c \ge 1$ ($c_{\mathrm{opt}}(d) = \lfloor d/2 \rfloor$), bypassed by the prismatic PD horizontal-section theorem (Thm.~\ref{thm:2:pd}).
\item \emph{$\FF_2[A_5]$-modular induction and degree-$21$ $\PSL_2(\FF_7)$ descent} (Prop.~\ref{prop:3:a5obs}, Thm.~\ref{thm:3:catalog}(v)).
\item \emph{Excluding \textup{\texttt{(1\^{}3 1)}} orbits via~\cite{ST2025a,ST2025b} to isolate squarefree $S_3$} (Obs.~\ref{obs:4:s3}(i)): hits the Taniguchi--Thorne~\cite{TT2020} $\theta(4,2)=5/6$ wall (no longer needed for \texttt{ch23} thanks to Thm.~\ref{thm:2:closure}).
\item \emph{Zero-preprint $S_3$ from $a=m^2$, elliptic points, or positive-density second moment} (Obs.~\ref{obs:4:s3}): bounded at $X^{7/8} < X^{23/24}$, $X^{3/4}$, and nonlinear $\dim W = \dim G = 20$.
\item \emph{Tame Hecke separation, LDS $(2e+1,2)$, and tame-level-$1$ $\GL_4$ Shalika} (Thm.~\ref{thm:5:rq-count1}(ii)).
\item \emph{$\zeta_\ell$-admissible Kolyvagin, Burnside--$\mu$, Bott non-transfer to $\GSpin(5,2)$, and untwisted $d_0 \ge 3$ channels} (Thm.~\ref{thm:5:d3obs}).
\item \emph{Brumer--Stark, BCG/Sczech, $\widetilde{H}^1$/Bianchi, Igusa towers, and all non-minimal reductive groups} (Thm.~\ref{thm:5:4pillar}, Thm.~\ref{thm:6:wallb}(ii)).
\item \emph{Golod--Shafarevich towers for \texttt{ChCore} \texttt{(hN2)} and $S$-ramified pro-$q$ towers for \texttt{h1}} (Thm.~\ref{thm:6:hostex}(i),(iii)).
\end{enumerate}
